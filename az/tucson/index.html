Hardness: how to get a real figure

Do not use a blog’s “Tucson is X grains.” Tucson Water says the aquifer + CAP blend varies across the service area, and hardness rises as the CAP share increases. SoftenerIndex leaves hardnessGpg and hardnessMgL unset.

To get a number you can hand a dealer:

  1. Open Tucson Water’s Hard Water page for the utility’s own description and home-management tips.
  2. Use the Water Quality hub links to the current Consumer Confidence Report and the Water Quality in Your Neighborhood Dashboard for address-level monitoring context.
  3. If a published document does not state hardness for your pressure zone or sample area in gpg or mg/L as CaCO₃, do not convert adjectives (“hard,” “very hard”) into grains. The parameter-explanation PDF’s Soft/Moderate/Hard/Very Hard bands are definitions, not a SoftenerIndex reading for your tap.
  4. A test at your tap (titration kit, pool kit, or lab) is the figure that actually sizes a resin bed. City-wide language is context, not a substitute.

When we attach a figure, it will appear in the data box on this page with the source name, URL, and retrieved date. If that box still says “not sourced,” there is no SoftenerIndex hardness number.

Finding an installer without a fake roster

Use the Arizona Registrar of Contractors public search (contractor search portal linked in sources). Confirm:

  • The legal name matches the quote.
  • The license class covers the work (residential/dual plumbing classifications such as R-37 or CR-37 as applicable).
  • The license is active on the day you sign.

Then run the quote through How to read a dealer quote. Demand brand, model, grain capacity, and a hardness input. Compare more than one written bid using the 2026 fair-quote guide.

Door-to-door is common in hard-water markets. It is not a credential.

Local questions that are not numbers

Ask Tucson-area bidders things that do not require SoftenerIndex to invent data:

  • Where will the tank sit (garage slab, closet, exterior), and what sun / dust / freeze exposure does that imply in the desert?
  • How will regeneration reach a drain, and has the bidder confirmed the utility / sewer district will accept brine?
  • Is the pitch salt-based ion exchange or a salt-free conditioner? Read salt-based vs salt-free if the language is muddy.
  • Who pulls any required permit?
  • If you run an evaporative cooler, what maintenance does the bidder assume — Tucson Water’s own hard-water tips call out cooler pads and in-line filters as separate household chores, not softener marketing.
