SoftenerIndex is Molina’s public notebook for people who were just told they need a water softener — usually by someone already standing in the garage.

The site’s job is narrow: explain what a fair quote contains, separate ion exchange from salt-free marketing, and keep city pages honest when the data file is still empty. It is not a dealer. It does not dispatch trucks. It does not invent a Phoenix hardness number to look finished.
